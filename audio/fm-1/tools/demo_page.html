Felucca firmware, played on the FM-1 emulator

__STATS__

The firmware runs unmodified in the Rust pi32v2 emulator. A script presses keys and turns knob 1 through the modelled key matrix and encoders. The screen frames and the sound below come straight out of the emulated LCD and the I2S DMA. Press play.

Front-panel script

The layout is schematic: the controls are Felucca's (panel.c), but their positions are not measured from the faceplate. The screen updates in 100 ms snapshots. The sound is the firmware's own render at 44.1 kHz, converted from 24-bit to 16-bit.

Command: fm1-emu --bin work/felucca_xip.bin --entry 0x02000120 --ms 7000 --wav work/demo.wav --keys "…" --snap 400,500,…
