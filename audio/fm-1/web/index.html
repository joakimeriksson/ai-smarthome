M-VAVE FM-1 (emulated)

click Start to boot

Mouse: click or drag across the keys, click buttons, drag a knob up / down or scroll over it; MASTER is the volume. Keyboard: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; ' = C4..F5 · Z / X = OCT− / OCT+ · Space = PLAY · R = REC · 1..0 = FX SCL ENV LFO EDIT GLO HOME SAVE ARP SEQ · ← → SELECT · ↑ ↓ PRESETS · , . ALGORITHM. The pi32v2 CPU, its peripherals and the LCD run in WebAssembly in the audio thread. The real FM-1 runs at 240 MHz; a lower clock keeps the emulation real-time on slower machines. Changing the firmware or the clock reboots the synth. The firmwares are the community's own builds, unchanged; the list is from fm1-editor.com/firmware.
