      How it works

      Six layers, from your speakers down to a 1982 sound chip. The page only builds the firmware image and moves keys; everything below the first layer is the FM-1’s own CPU at work.

      1. Your browserAudioWorkletAsks for 128 frames at a time. The emulator runs until the FM-1’s I2S DMA has produced them, so the sound card is the clock.
      2. fm1-coreRust → WebAssemblyThe JieLi AC791N’s pi32v2 CPU with its interrupts, timers, I2S, the SPI screen and the key matrix, worked out from the stock firmware. Idle time is skipped, not emulated.
      3. The player firmwareC · 196 KBRuns on Felucca’s hardware layer. The tunes ride after it in flash as a tune pack, which this page builds from your playlist and boots.
      4. A 6502floooh’s m6502Each tune’s own init and play routines, cycle by cycle, in 64 KB of C64 memory with the I/O banking, the CIA timer and the raster line.
      5. SID writescycle-stampedThe main loop runs the tune up to three frames ahead and queues every register write with the C64 cycle it happened on.
      6. reSID 0.16Dag LemClocked to each write’s cycle inside the audio interrupt, with measured 6581 and 8580 waveforms and filters. Or fm1-sid’s own integer model, at about 70 % of the cost.
      9.5 %of the 240 MHz core goes to the audio interrupt with reSID
      0.984correlation with reSID on a PC, fed the same register stream
      196 KBof firmware: player, two SID engines, the screen and its own tune
      61,157tunes in HVSC, one search away

      The numbers come from the emulator, which counts instructions, not cycles: a real FM-1 has not run this firmware yet. The status line under the synth shows how fast your machine runs it.

      Controls

      The firmware reads the FM-1’s own controls; the buttons above press them for you.

      On the FM-1

      PRESETS or SELECT knob, OCT− / OCT+: tune. PLAY: pause. EDIT: reSID or the own model. FX: 6581 or 8580. Drag a knob up or down, or scroll over it.

      Keyboard

      ↑ ↓ ← → or N P: tune · Space: pause · 5: EDIT · 1: FX · Z X: OCT− / OCT+
